You can book appointments with me here. The service is provided by [Fantastical Openings](https://flexibits.com/fantastical/help/openings) and syncs with my calendar. After you send the request, I will get a notification to approve the meeting time. The service will then generate a Webex meeting automatically and send you the meeting details. Nice, huh?

- [one-hour meetings](https://fantastical.app/jpwiedekopf-y4FA/1-hour)
- [two-hour meetings](https://fantastical.app/jpwiedekopf-y4FA/2-hour)
